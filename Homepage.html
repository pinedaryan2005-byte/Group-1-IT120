FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Here are answers to some common questions about solar energy and our services.

How does solar energy work?

Solar panels capture sunlight and convert it into electricity that can be used to power homes and businesses.

Can solar power reduce my electricity bill?

Solar energy can reduce your dependence on electricity from the grid and may help lower your electricity expenses.

Do solar panels require maintenance?

Solar systems require periodic inspection and maintenance to help keep them operating efficiently.

How can I request a solar consultation?

You can contact Sun Son Solar through our support section or request a free quote through our website.
